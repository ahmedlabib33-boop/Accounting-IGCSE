# Teacher and examiner review: Cambridge IGCSE Accounting 0452 (2027–2029)

Every lesson, study guide, exercise, question bank item, mock paper and Practice Lab drill in the app was
reviewed against the official 2027–2029 syllabus (text in `docs/syllabus/0452-2027-2029-syllabus.txt`) the way
a senior 0452 teacher and examiner would before students use it. Corrections were made in place. The detailed
change logs, one row per change with a coverage checklist against every syllabus bullet, are in
`data/accounting/deep/reviews/`.

## Standards now applied everywhere

- Official terminology: statement of profit or loss, statement of financial position, statement of changes in
  equity, allowance for irrecoverable debts, irrecoverable debts recovered, provision for depreciation,
  carrying amount, other receivables and payables, accounting concepts, inter-business comparison, interested
  parties.
- The ten syllabus ratios with the only formulas Cambridge accepts, including ROCE on profit for the year before
  interest and capital employed = issued shares + reserves + non-current liabilities.
- Official topic and section titles.
- Every Paper 2 style question ends with an evaluate or advise part, because evaluation (AO3) is about 15% of
  Paper 2.
- Every multiple-choice question has exactly one defensible answer and distractors that are real student errors.
- Mark points are one creditable item each, with own-figure follow-through where an examiner would allow it.
- Nothing outside the syllabus (no partnership goodwill, admission or dissolution; incomplete records only for
  sole-trader trading businesses; no specific software packages).
