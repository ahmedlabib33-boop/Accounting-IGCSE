# Cambridge 0452 curriculum map

Exam years: 2027–2029. Scope authority: [official syllabus](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/718141-2027-2029-syllabus.pdf). This map describes independently written material; it does not reproduce a Cambridge textbook.

| Sections | Instruction and worked methods included |
|---|---|
| 1.1–1.2 | Recording versus interpretation; performance and cash; decisions; resources, obligations, owner funds; transaction effects and the equation |
| 2.1 | Debit/credit rules; complete posting sequences; T accounts and running balances; balancing, closing and transfer; ledger divisions; digital records |
| 2.2 | Invoices, debit and credit notes, receipts, cheque counterfoils, deposit evidence, bank and customer statements; paper and digital evidence |
| 2.3 | Cash/petty cash and journals; posting totals and personal accounts; trade and settlement discounts; contra entries; card/online payments; imprest; cash risk; manual and digital records |
| 3.1 | Trial-balance purpose, preparation from balances and limitations; omission, commission, principle, reversal, original-entry and compensating errors |
| 3.2 | Correcting journals; suspense sides and wrong-side corrections; consequences for profit and financial position |
| 3.3 | Cash-book updates; charges, interest, standing orders, direct debits and transfers; timing items and bank errors; overdrafts and digital reconciliation |
| 3.4 | Both control accounts; source totals; receipts/payments, credit activity, discounts, returns, debts, dishonoured receipts, interest, contra and refunds; unusual balances and digital limitations |
| 4.1 | Capital/revenue spending and receipts; classification; correction effects on assets and profit |
| 4.2 | Straight-line, reducing-balance and revaluation depreciation; selection of method; purchases, accumulated depreciation, disposal accounts, gains and losses |
| 4.3 | Accrued/prepaid costs and income; journal and closing-balance treatment; opening adjustments and matched annual amounts |
| 4.4 | Write-offs, recovery, allowance creation/increase/decrease, net receivables and profit effects |
| 4.5 | Cost/NRV, item-level comparison and inventory errors across periods |
| 5.1 | Sole ownership; trading, services and manufacturing contexts; profit or loss and dated financial position; intangible/current/non-current assets and liabilities; year-end adjustments; goods/private-expense drawings |
| 5.2 | Partnership benefits/limitations and agreement; operating profit versus appropriation; loan/capital/drawings interest, salary, profit/loss shares; fixed capital and current balances; financial-position presentation |
| 5.3 | Separate entity and limited liability; company benefits/costs; ordinary/preference shares and debentures; issued/called/paid capital; profit, equity movements, reserves, retained earnings and position |
| 5.4 | Direct/indirect production costs; materials consumed, wages, direct expenses, prime cost, factory overheads, WIP; completed output, finished-goods trading and financial position |
| 5.5 | Receipts/payments versus matched income/expenditure; subscription adjustments; refreshments trading; surplus/deficit and accumulated fund; asset/period adjustments |
| 5.6 | Reasons and risks of partial records; statements of affairs; owner-adjusted capital movements; reconstruction using control workings, margins, mark-up and turnover; adjusted trading statements |
| 6.1 | Gross margin, mark-up, profit margin, ROCE and specified capital employed; current/acid-test ratios; inventory times/days and credit collection/payment days |
| 6.2 | Changes over time; price/cost/inventory/expense effects; cash-profit differences; profitability, liquidity and working-capital actions and trade-offs |
| 6.3–6.5 | Industry, size, policy and period differences; stakeholder decisions; historic-cost, policy and non-financial limitations |
| 7.1 | Entity, duality, continuity, cost, matching, realisation, consistency, prudence, significance and monetary measurement, with applied examples |
| 7.2 | Integrity, objectivity, competence/care, confidentiality and professional behaviour; conflicts, pressure, stakeholder impact and appropriate responses |
| 7.3 | Digital processing and its limits; paper, local-device and cloud storage; access, backups, restoration, retention and sustainable use |

Assessment practice includes calculations, ledger and journal preparation, cash-book reconciliation, adjusted statements, appropriations, production, club accounts and interpretation/evaluation. The revision handbook adds a response checklist. Official resource links are maintained separately so that original app practice cannot be mistaken for official papers.

To review completeness at objective level, compare the official subject-content pages with this map and the actual lesson depth. The automated section-count test alone cannot make that judgement.
